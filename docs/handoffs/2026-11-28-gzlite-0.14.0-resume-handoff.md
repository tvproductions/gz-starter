repository to [tvproductions/gzlite](https://github.com/tvproductions/gzlite)
after candidate verification. The published baseline for this work was
GZ Starter 0.13.0. The Task 14 checkpoint merged in PR #22 at
`3ca3f96aaee59c16fec5b9506a183618887d977e`.

annotated `v0.14.0` tag and release record. The
[adopter bridge](../migrating-from-gz-starter.md) gives the approved
migration and verification sequence.

The prior [cross-machine checkpoint](2026-11-28-gzlite-0.14.0-cross-machine-checkpoint.md)
records the earlier Tasks 10 and 11 evidence. Tasks 15–17 and 12 add and
qualify the producer guards, tracking, and installed adopter bridge.

activation. Preserve existing rules and published tags.

[planned follow-up](../superpowers/plans/2026-11-27-gzlite-0.14.0.md#task-13-gzlite-0141-after-gz-skills-v0150-is-released)
