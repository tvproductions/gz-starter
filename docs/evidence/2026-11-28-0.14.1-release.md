  maximum of 20, weighted mean 5.66 and no ceiling violations. Vulture 2.16
  at confidence 90, deptry 0.25.1 and Import Linter 2.17 passed.

  branch-enabled report shows 73% aggregate coverage for `src/gzlite`.
  Verifier modules exercised in child processes are outside that measurement
  and are covered separately by the CLI tests and installed-artifact gate.
- Cosmic Ray 8.9.0 passed the bounded synthetic unittest probe. Structured
  results recorded two killed and two surviving mutations with normal
  workers. The failing baseline, timeout and launch error were distinguished.
  The launch error returned zero and recorded an incompetent test outcome.
  This checks mutation tooling compatibility in the selected environment.

  verifier tests reject unsafe input. No high findings were reported.
  These diagnostics remain visible. Some locked launchers failed on the
  mapped drive and were rerun from a local temporary environment.
